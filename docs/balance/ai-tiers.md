# AI tier head-to-head

Classic rules, colours alternated, paired dice seeds. Generated by `tool/ai_tourney.dart`.

| A | B | Games | A win rate (95% CI) | A ms/move |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| master | hard | 2000 | 70.8% ± 2.0 | 22.9 ms |
| hard | medium | 2000 | 67.7% ± 2.1 | 0.6 ms |
| medium | easy | 1000 | 60.3% ± 3.0 | 0.5 ms |
| easy | beginner | 1000 | 89.3% ± 1.9 | 0.3 ms |
| master p=1 | hard | 1000 | 64.8% ± 3.0 | 0.8 ms |

Targets: Master ≥ 65% vs Hard and Hard ≥ 62% vs Medium over ≥ 2,000 games, ~30 ms per move on the server. Both are met.

* Master: self-play value network (`lib/core/ai/nn_weights.dart`, 300k TD(0) games) + 2-ply over its 5 best candidates, opening book.
* Hard: hand-tuned evaluator, no noise, opening book.
* Medium / Easy / Beginner: hand evaluator with 0.15 / 0.3 / 0.5 uniform noise (Beginner also plays 40% random moves).
* "master p=1" (network without search) shows the network alone is worth ~65%; search adds ~6 points.

Reproduce: `dart compile exe tool/ai_tourney.dart -o build/tourney && build/tourney --a master --b hard --games 2000`.
