# HaftNard — store listing (English)

**App name (max 30):**
HaftNard: Backgammon & Cards

**Short description (max 80):**
Persian backgammon with a magic Double Seven and power cards. Offline, 2 players

**Full description:**
HaftNard is the backgammon you know, with a twist: sometimes the dice land on a glowing Double Seven and you choose one of three power cards!

🎲 Double Seven and 80 power cards
Fog the opponent's dice, freeze checkers, raise a holy shield, hide a mine, stop time and much more. Every power shows on the board with its own visual and sound.

⚙️ Set up your game
"Seven" mode with cards or pure "Classic", Double Seven chance from low to very high, matches of 1 to 7 points and an optional doubling cube.

🤖 A smart computer opponent
Five levels from Beginner to Master. A random opponent with its own style each game. Master plays with a neural network and perfect bear-offs.

👥 Play with a friend
On one phone, or on two phones over the same Wi-Fi or a hotspot; no internet or server needed.

🗺️ Journey
A roguelike road with 15 bosses, 60 talismans, a bazaar and a daily run.

🎨 Beautiful and Persian
9 boards, 7 environments, many checkers and dice, adaptive Persian music (Mahur, Shur, Homayoun), full Persian and English.

No ads, no data collection, fully offline.
